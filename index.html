ABOUT DMD

Built around the value of a good property.

DMD Strategic Solutions LLC is a growing real estate investment company focused on the buy–remodel–sell model, with the flexibility to hold properties as long-term investments when the opportunity makes sense.

Our approach is simple: identify opportunity, improve the property, control costs, and create value.
